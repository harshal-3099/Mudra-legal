Privacy Policy

Last updated: 03rd Feb 2026

Mudra (“the App”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the App.

1. Information Collection

The App does not collect any personal data.

No user accounts

No sign-in

No analytics

No ads

No cloud storage

No third-party SDKs

No external servers

All information entered into the App remains only on your device.

2. Local Data Storage

Expense records are stored locally on your device

Data never leaves your phone

The developer has no access to your data

If the App is uninstalled or app data is cleared, all stored information is permanently deleted.

3. Permissions Used
SMS Read Permission (Optional)

The App may request permission to read SMS messages only if the user enables this feature.

This permission is used exclusively to:

Detect expense-related SMS (such as bank or transaction alerts)

Automatically categorize expenses for user convenience

Important clarifications:

SMS access is optional

The App works without granting this permission

SMS content is not stored externally

SMS data is never shared, uploaded, or transmitted

4. Data Sharing

The App does not share data with:

Third parties

Advertisers

Analytics services

Government or legal entities

Because no data is collected or transmitted, data sharing does not occur.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for users 16 years of age and above.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

6. Security

Since all data is stored locally:

Data security depends on your device’s security

Users are encouraged to protect their devices with PINs, passwords, or biometrics

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally.
Any changes will be reflected within the App or its store listing.

Continued use of the App indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
